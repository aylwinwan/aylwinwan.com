Life Design Principles

Build life intentionally.

Eight life design principles that help design a life with greater intention.

Build life
intentionally.

Curiosity

Some boundaries exist only until we question them and venture beyond.

Context

The present becomes clearer when we understand what came before and the forces that shaped it.

Wonder

The world holds more than our experience has taught us to imagine.

Belonging

Where we belong need not be where we began; the places and people we choose help shape who we become.

Shared Experiences

What we experience together becomes part of the life we build together.

Presence

In being fully there with one another, ordinary moments become part of who we are and what makes life meaningful.

Season

Some possibilities belong to a season of life; what matters is also a question of when.

Options

The capabilities and resources we build today give our future selves greater freedom to choose—to move, to stay, or to begin again.
